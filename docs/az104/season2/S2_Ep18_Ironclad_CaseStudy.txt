</Person2><Person1> "Seven steps. One: read the question first, then the case study, so you know what to look for. Two: while reading, build a requirements table, in your own words, with the exact verbs and nouns: must, must not, only, exclusively, minimize. </Person2><Person1> "Trap one, unstated services, like our Table and File.
